What a sauna needs before it can be installed

Four things decide whether a sauna goes in smoothly: the power, the floor, the way in, and the building. None of them is expensive to check, and all of them are cheaper to check first.

Dubai, UAE · Updated November 2026

A sauna is not a complicated installation, but it is an installation, not a delivery. Everything below is what we check on the site visit, written out so you can check it yourself first if you would rather.

1. The electrical supply

The single most important item, and the only one that genuinely stops a job.

Our saunas are configurable for a single-phase supply, up to 8.5 kW. The two-person cabin runs a Harvia 4.5 kW heater and the four-person a Harvia 6.0 kW. You provide a suitable isolator near where the sauna will stand, ready for connection by our installation team.

What to ask a licensed electrician for:

  • A dedicated circuit for the sauna, sized for the heater we specify
  • An isolator switch within reach of the cabin, outside it
  • For an outdoor cabin, enclosures and glands rated for outdoor use
  • Confirmation that the board has the headroom, with everything else in the home running

Get this quoted before you order a sauna. If the nearest board is three rooms away, the electrical run can be a meaningful cost, and it is your electrician's cost, not ours, so it will not appear on our quotation.

2. The floor

What the cabin stands on needs to be level, solid and able to take the load. For an outdoor cabin that usually means an existing terrace, a paved area or a roof deck. For an indoor one, an ordinary tiled or screeded floor.

Two things we look for:

  • Level. A door that will not close squarely is almost always a floor that is out by a few millimetres. This is fixable. It is easier to know beforehand.
  • Drainage, if it is outside. Not for the sauna, which produces no run-off, but for where you will cool down.

On a balcony or a roof, the question becomes structural. We give you the dimensions and the weight. If the building wants a structural opinion, those are the numbers it starts from. See can you put a sauna in an apartment or on a balcony.

3. The route in

The thing clients think of last and we think of first. A cabin is either carried in assembled or built in place, and which one depends on the narrowest point between the street and the spot.

What we measure: the lift, the stairwell turn, the door widths, the balcony threshold, any gate on the way. Built in place takes longer but is entirely routine. The point is to know which job it is before the day.

4. The building's permission

Developers, building managements and community rules differ across Dubai, and they are not something a sauna company can speak for. Some care about anything visible from outside. Some care about electrical work in a leased unit. Some have no view at all.

Ask early. Before a deposit, not after. We give you the dimensions, the weight and the electrical load, which is usually all your building management needs to give you an answer.

What the installation day actually looks like

Our own team, not a subcontractor. They bring the cabin or the parts, protect the route, build or place it, connect to the isolator your electrician has left, commission the heater, and run it up to temperature with you there.

Then a walkthrough: how to start it, how long it takes, what to do with the stones, how to air it out. Ten minutes, and it is most of what you will ever need to know. Everything is covered by five years on workmanship and two years manufacturer's warranty on the electrical components.

What we need from you

Honestly, very little:

  • The isolator in place and ready
  • The route clear on the day
  • Any building approval already obtained
  • Someone there who can make a decision if something needs deciding

How long the whole thing takes

A site visit first. A fixed-price quotation after it. The cabin, the heater, the controls, any options, one number. Then the build, and then the installation. We give you the dates with the quotation, once we have actually seen the space. We do not quote dates before that, because a quotation made without seeing the room is a guess with a number attached.

Dubai is included in our prices. Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Al Ain and beyond the UAE carry an additional transport and installation fee, quoted by location.

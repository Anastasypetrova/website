How much does a sauna cost in Dubai?

Most sauna companies in the UAE will not put a number on a page. Here are ours, what they include, and how they sit against everyone else selling a sauna in Dubai right now.

Dubai, UAE · Updated November 2026

A sauna in Dubai costs between AED 15,000 and AED 25,000 for a home cabin, fully installed. A two-person sauna starts at AED 15,000, a four-person at AED 20,000, and a sauna built around a room you already have starts at AED 25,000. Those are our prices, and they include everything: the cabin, the Harvia heater, delivery and installation by our own team.

The wide spread you see elsewhere, and the number of companies who will only say “request a quote”, is the reason this page exists.

What is included in the price

Every price above is turnkey. Nothing arrives in a flat pack for someone else to assemble, and there is no separate installation invoice at the end. Included:

  • The cabin itself, built by us
  • A Harvia heater, sized to the cabin
  • Delivery to the address
  • Build and installation by our own installation team
  • A five-year warranty on workmanship, and two years manufacturer's warranty on the electrical components
  • A walkthrough on the day, so you know how to run it and look after it

What is not included is the electrical supply up to the sauna. You provide a suitable isolator, ready for our team to connect. That is a licensed electrician's job, and a small one. What a sauna needs before installation covers exactly what to ask them for.

What everyone else charges

We looked at what was publicly listed by sauna sellers in the UAE in November 2026. Outdoor cabins were advertised between roughly AED 21,000 and AED 44,000, with most of the Finnish-style cabins clustered in the mid-twenties to low thirties. Several companies list no price at all and ask you to request a quote.

So our two-person cabin at AED 15,000 sits below the bottom of that published range, and the four-person at AED 20,000 is at or under it. We are not a reseller importing finished cabins. We design and build them here, which is where the difference goes.

Those figures move, and some listings were old when we read them. Check them yourself before you take our word for it. That is rather the point of putting ours in writing.

What changes the price

Within each size, the things that move the number are:

  • Where it goes. A ground-floor terrace with a clear path is the simplest case. A roof terrace reached by a service lift, or a cabin that has to be built in place because it cannot be carried in assembled, takes longer.
  • The electrical run. If the isolator is already near where the sauna will stand, there is nothing to do. If the nearest board is three rooms away, your electrician's cost goes up, not ours.
  • Glass. A full glass front costs more than a glazed door, and changes how the cabin reads in the space.
  • Controls. The panel options above.
  • Custom work. A sauna built into an existing alcove, shower or gym corner starts at AED 25,000 because every one is drawn from scratch.

What it costs to run

Less than almost everyone expects. A 4.5 kW heater draws 4.5 kW while it is heating and considerably less once it is holding temperature, so a typical session lands in the single digits of dirhams. We worked the numbers through the DEWA slabs in what a home sauna costs to run in Dubai.

Maintenance is close to nothing: wipe the benches, let the cabin air out, keep the heater stones clear.

Does it work in the summer?

Yes, and the summer helps rather than hurts. A cabin that starts at 40°C reaches working temperature faster than one that starts at 18°C. The honest answer is longer than that, and it is in does an outdoor sauna work in the Dubai summer.

How to get a fixed number

We quote after a site visit, not before. Someone comes out, measures the space, looks at where the power is and how the sauna would get in, and you get one fixed price for the whole job. It does not move afterwards.

Dubai is included. Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Al Ain and further afield are served with an additional transport and installation fee, quoted by location.

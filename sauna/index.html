The Science

Heat, cold, and the body.

A practice of contrast. What happens inside when you sit in 90°C dry heat, step into 5°C water, and do it again tomorrow evening.

01 · Recovery

Less soreness, faster return.

Sauna and cold-water immersion after training are associated with lower perceived soreness and better next-day performance. In a city where most people train at 6 a.m. or 9 p.m., recovery matters as much as the session.

02 · Resilience

Trained stress, trained calm.

Deliberate, controlled heat and cold train the nervous system to enter stress fast and leave it faster. A steadier baseline — built by practice, not willpower.

03 · Ritual

A practice you can keep.

Sleep, mood, focus. The real case for heat and cold isn’t one dramatic session — it’s the compounding effect of showing up four evenings a week, for a year, twenty steps from your own bedroom.

Notes

Notes from the practice.

Building · 4 min read

Can a wooden sauna survive a Dubai summer?

Yes — if it is built for one. Timber that holds its shape in dry heat, a ventilated shell, a UV-stable finish outside, and a heater sized for a room that starts at 42°C rather than 20°C.

Heaters · 3 min read

Why we fit Harvia heaters.

Finnish, serviceable, and sized to the cabin: 4.5 kW for two people, 6.0 kW for four. Parts are available here, which matters more than any spec sheet on the day something needs replacing.

Protocols · 5 min read

An evening protocol for heat and cold.

Fifteen minutes at 85–90°C. Two minutes in the plunge at 5°C. Rest until the shivering stops. Repeat three times, and finish with the cold. Water in between, no phone, lights low.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

What power supply does a sauna need?

Our saunas are configurable for a single-phase supply, up to 8.5 kW. The two-person runs a Harvia 4.5 kW heater, the four-person a 6.0 kW. You provide a suitable isolator, ready for connection by our installation team.

Is the installation included?

Yes. Every sauna is turnkey: full supply, build and installation by our own team. Nothing is dropped off in a box for someone else to assemble.

Can you fit a sauna into a room I already have?

That is what the custom build is for. Dimensions are tailored to your space and the capacity to suit you. We measure on the site visit and quote from there, starting at AED 25,000.

What is a hybrid sauna?

Infrared light panels added on request, so one cabin gives you traditional Finnish heat and infrared. Ask for it at the quotation stage.

Can I control the sauna from my phone?

Internal Harvia controls are standard. An external control panel is AED 2,000, and a WiFi panel, which lets you start it from your phone, is AED 4,000.

What does the warranty cover?

Five years on workmanship, and two years manufacturer's warranty on the electrical components.

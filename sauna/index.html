The Science

Heat and the body.

What ninety minutes a week at 90°C does, and why the people who keep a sauna at home end up using it more than they expected.

01 · Recovery

Less soreness, faster return.

Sitting in dry heat after training is associated with lower perceived soreness and better output the next day. In a city where most people train at 6 a.m. or 9 p.m., recovery decides how the next session goes.

02 · Sleep

Warm, then cooling.

Core temperature rises in the sauna and falls afterwards, and that fall is the signal the body reads as time to sleep. An evening session an hour or two before bed is the one most of our clients settle into.

03 · Ritual

A practice you can keep.

The case for a sauna at home is not one dramatic session. It is the compounding of four evenings a week, for a year, twenty steps from your own bedroom, with nothing to book and nowhere to drive.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

How much does a sauna cost in Dubai?

A two-person sauna starts at AED 15,000 and a four-person at AED 20,000, both turnkey: supply, build and installation. A sauna built around an existing space starts at AED 25,000, quoted after a site visit.

What size sauna do I need?

The two-person cabin is 1.2 × 1.2 × 2.2 m inside and takes two people sitting. The four-person is 1.8 × 1.25 × 2.2 m, with a bench long enough to lie on. If neither fits the space you have, we build to your dimensions.

Can you build a sauna to fit my room?

Yes — that is what the custom build is for. An unused shower, a spare alcove, a corner of the gym: we measure it, design the sauna around it and build it to fit. From AED 25,000.

Does an outdoor sauna work in the Dubai summer?

Our cabins stand outdoors on terraces, roofs and beside pools across Dubai. A room that starts at 40°C reaches working temperature faster, not slower; most clients use theirs after sunset.

Can a sauna be installed in an apartment or on a balcony?

Often yes. What decides it is the floor, the power supply and what your building allows. We check all three at the site visit before quoting anything.

Do I need approval from my building or developer?

That depends on the building, and communities differ. Ask your developer or building management early; we give you the dimensions, the weight and the electrical load they will ask for.

What power supply does a sauna need?

Our saunas are configurable for a single-phase supply, up to 8.5 kW. The two-person runs a Harvia 4.5 kW heater, the four-person a 6.0 kW. You provide a suitable isolator, ready for connection by our installation team.

How much electricity does a home sauna use?

A 4.5 kW heater draws 4.5 kW while it is heating and considerably less once it is holding temperature. Multiply by your DEWA tariff and the hours you actually use it; for most households it is a small line on the bill.

How long does a sauna take to heat up?

Usually half an hour to forty-five minutes for a cabin this size, less in summer when it starts warm. With the WiFi panel you start it from your phone and walk into it ready.

How long does the whole process take?

A site visit first, a fixed-price quotation after it, then the build and the installation. We give you the dates with the quotation, once we have seen the space.

Is installation included in the price?

Yes. Every sauna is turnkey: full supply, build and installation by our own team. Nothing is dropped off in a box for someone else to assemble.

What is a hybrid sauna, and how is infrared different?

A traditional sauna heats the air, and you feel the room. Infrared panels warm you directly at a lower air temperature. A hybrid has both in one cabin — we add the infrared panels on request.

Can I turn the sauna on from my phone?

With the WiFi control panel, yes — it is AED 4,000. Internal Harvia controls are standard, and an external panel is AED 2,000.

What maintenance does a sauna need?

Very little. Wipe the benches down, let the cabin air out after use, and keep the heater stones clear. We show you all of it on the day we hand it over.

What does the warranty cover?

Five years on workmanship, and two years manufacturer’s warranty on the electrical components.

Do you deliver outside Dubai?

Dubai is included. Abu Dhabi, Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Al Ain and beyond the UAE are served with an additional transport and installation fee, quoted by location.

The Science

Heat, cold, and the body.

A practice of contrast. What happens inside when you sit in 90°C dry heat, step into 5°C water, and do it again tomorrow evening.

01 · Recovery

Less soreness, faster return.

Sauna and cold-water immersion after training are associated with lower perceived soreness and better next-day performance. In a city where most people train at 6 a.m. or 9 p.m., recovery matters as much as the session.

02 · Resilience

Trained stress, trained calm.

Deliberate, controlled heat and cold train the nervous system to enter stress fast and leave it faster. A steadier baseline — built by practice, not willpower.

03 · Ritual

A practice you can keep.

Sleep, mood, focus. The real case for heat and cold isn’t one dramatic session — it’s the compounding effect of showing up four evenings a week, for a year, twenty steps from your own bedroom.

Notes

Notes from the practice.

Building · 4 min read

Can a wooden sauna survive a Dubai summer?

Yes — if it is built for one. Thermo-treated wood that no longer absorbs moisture, a ventilated double shell, UV-stable oil on the outside, and a heater sized for a room that starts at 42°C rather than 20°C.

Materials · 3 min read

Why we use thermo-aspen, not just cedar.

Cedar smells wonderful and looks the part. Thermo-aspen stays lighter, doesn’t drip resin on your back at 90°C, and holds its shape in dry desert air. Most of our builds use both, in the right places.

Protocols · 5 min read

An evening protocol for heat and cold.

Fifteen minutes at 85–90°C. Two minutes in the plunge at 5°C. Rest until the shivering stops. Repeat three times, and finish with the cold. Water in between, no phone, lights low.

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Does an outdoor sauna really work in the UAE heat?

Yes. Our outdoor cabins are built with an insulated double shell, thermo-treated wood and a UV-stable finish. In summer the room warms up faster, not worse. Most clients sit after sunset, when the contrast with the cold plunge is at its best.

What power supply does a sauna need?

A typical 2–4 seat sauna runs a 6–9 kW heater, larger cabins 9–15 kW, usually on a dedicated three-phase line. We check your DB board at the site visit and coordinate the connection with a licensed electrical contractor.

How long does it take?

Design takes about a week. Building takes four to six weeks in our workshop. Installation is two to three days on site. From deposit to your first session, plan on six to eight weeks.

Do you work in Abu Dhabi?

Yes — Dubai and Abu Dhabi are both included, with no extra delivery or installation charge. Sharjah, Ras Al Khaimah, Al Ain and the wider GCC are quoted by location.

What about maintenance, dust and humidity?

Very little. Wipe the benches, ventilate after use, re-oil the exterior once a year. We include a maintenance visit in the first year, and the chiller on a cold plunge has a washable filter you can change in a minute.

What is the warranty and how do I pay?

Two years on the structure and heater, one year on electronics, with our own team doing any service. Payment is 50% at design sign-off and 50% on the day of installation. Bank transfer or card.
